## Talabat destination and audience

[Talabat Egypt](https://www.talabat.com/egypt) is a customer food/grocery delivery surface. Its **Order food** link points to [Egypt restaurants](https://www.talabat.com/egypt/restaurants). Its footer links concrete customer restaurant pages, including [Taboon Zamalek](https://www.talabat.com/egypt/taboon-zamalek), and separately identifies partner onboarding at `eg.partner.talabat.com`. This distinguishes customer destinations from restaurant-management/onboarding destinations.

The provider should preserve an owner-supplied HTTPS merchant/share URL, rather than derive one from a restaurant name or phone number. Direct desktop navigation to `https://www.talabat.com/egypt/taboon-zamalek` rendered **“Taboon delivery service in Egypt | Talabat”**, an H1 **“Taboon”**, dishes/reviews, and **“Select your area to see the restaurant menu”**. Thus that exact URL is verified as a public restaurant page. The English observed pattern is `https://www.talabat.com/egypt/<actual-restaurant-slug>`; the page's language control points to `https://www.talabat.com/ar/egypt/taboon-zamalek`. This describes observed paths, not permission to invent slugs or acceptance of every matching path. The Egypt homepage and category/list pages are discovery sources, not acceptable substitutes for the business page. Taboon is a research example, not an endorsement, a fixture business's destination, or a verified installed-app handoff. Talabat's homepage emits its own app metadata and an Adjust store redirect; those are not a documented merchant deep-link API and are not reproduced or synthesized in NexTap.
